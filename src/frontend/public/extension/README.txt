Copie Past-e Smart Post Extension v1.0.0
==========================================

INSTALL STEPS:
1. Extract this zip to a folder on your computer.
2. Open Chrome and go to chrome://extensions
3. Enable Developer Mode (toggle in the top right corner).
4. Click 'Load unpacked' and select the extracted folder.
5. The Copie Past-e lightning bolt icon appears in your toolbar.
6. Open any listing in Copie Past-e and click Auto-Fill.

HOW IT WORKS:
- Open any saved listing in Copie Past-e
- Click 'Auto-Fill Facebook' or 'Auto-Fill OfferUp'
- The extension opens the marketplace and fills all fields automatically
- Review the pre-filled form, then click Post

WHAT GETS FILLED:
- Title
- Price
- Description
- Category (best-effort match)
- Images (up to 5)

If any field cannot be filled automatically, a banner at the top of the
page will list which fields need manual attention.

IMPORTANT:
- You must be logged into Facebook/OfferUp in your browser
- The extension never reads or stores your passwords or login info
- Facebook and OfferUp may update their forms periodically — if a field
  fails, fill it manually and the banner will show which fields need attention
- The extension works on Chrome only (Safari and Firefox coming soon)
