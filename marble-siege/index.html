THE GAME

Construct a fortress from colorful interlocking bricks. Deploy green toy soldiers. Launch glass marbles at the opposing fort, and send every enemy soldier tumbling.

HOW TO PLAY

  1. BUILD: Select a brick type, then tap a supported spot on the blue team's fort to place it. Use SOLDIERS to reposition your army.
  2. DEFEND: Different bricks have different durability. Reinforced blocks are limited.
  3. AIM: During your turn, adjust ANGLE and POWER. You can also drag the center launcher to change the angle.
  4. ATTACK: Tap FIRE. A direct marble hit can eliminate a soldier; collapsing the fort also works.
  5. PROGRESS: Eliminate the red army to win. Earn credits for enemy knockouts and victories. Buy better ammo between rounds.
